
## Individual Development Plan

This is a template IDP that you will fill out in your first year and we will revisit annually to update and discuss. This was largely taken from the University of Nebraska-Lincoln's template from the Institute of Agriculture and Natural Resources. 


### Self assessment 

1. Identify your past accomplishments since we last met that will contribute to the success of your postdoctoral training (e.g., publications, presentations, patents, grants, honors/awards, involvement in professional associations, courses taught). Identify how these accomplishments will contribute to your success.

### Career goals

1. What are your career goals? What position would you like to hold after the completion of your postdoctoral training? How will you use the postdoctoral training to help you achieve that position? What other types of positions have you considered? Indicate the degree to which you are enthusiastic about each and how the postdoctoral training could help prepare you for them.

```


```


2. When do you anticipate beginning a job search? What issues might affect the timing of your job search?


```


```


3. What specific areas of research do you wish to pursue during your postdoctoral training? Which research methods or approaches would you like to develop expertise in?

```


```


4. What professional skills would you like to develop during your postdoctoral training? What experiences do you think you will need to develop these skills? Be as specific as possible with your plan for developing these skills.

5. What are the primary factors driving your vision for the future and the goals you have identified above? Motivating factors could include personal interest in a particular research area, teaching skill, desire to have an impact, financial security, family concerns, place where you want to live and work, etc.
